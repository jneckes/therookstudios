What changed

Winners renew. Creators compete. The middle gets squeezed.

01 · Fewer, bigger, returning

Buyers renew what works and order fewer new formats.

Ampere puts “peak format” for the big streamers at 2022. Since then they have renewed their best performers and cut new format orders. Peacock says The Traitors has drawn 8.1 billion minutes since its fourth season premiered, and has renewed it through season nine.

Ampere · Peacock

02 · Creators are in the room

Creators are now partners, and competitors.

Ms. Rachel was Netflix’s most-watched kids title in the first half of 2026, with 69 million views. California’s first tax credit for a competition series went to a show from YouTube creator Mark Rober and Jimmy Kimmel.

Netflix · Office of the Governor of California

03 · The middle is squeezed

Scale is consolidating. Budget is no guarantee.

Banijay and All3Media closed their merger in July 2026, with €4.3 billion in combined revenue. Reality shoot days in Los Angeles fell 40% in the second quarter. And a second season of Beast Games, with a reported budget above $150 million, ranked 135th of 158 second seasons in one analyst’s tally of Nielsen charts.

Banijay · FilmLA via TheWrap · Entertainment Strategy Guy

Buyers aren’t buying less unscripted. They’re buying fewer, sharper shows from studios that know exactly what they make.

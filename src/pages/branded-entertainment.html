Ownership

Four steps from fee to owned IP.

1

Branded production

Fee plus markup. The brand owns it.

2

Back-end deals

A share of licensing, plus format rights.

3

Co-owned IP

Owned with a brand or talent partner.

4

Studio originals

You own it. Brands sponsor, platforms license.

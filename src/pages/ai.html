Why data first

Every show teaches the next one.

Off-the-shelf AI does the same thing on day 1,000 as on day one. A system built on your studio’s own history improves with use. Every pitch adds who passed and why. Every season adds what rated, what cut through, and what the buyer asked for next.

That knowledge stays with the studio when people move on. It makes the next pitch sharper and the next production easier, and nobody else has it.

The buyer map

“Which buyers bought a competition format like ours in the last 18 months, and who can walk us in?”

  • Every pitch, pass note and deal the studio has logged
  • Email and calendar: who we talk to, and how recently
  • Trade coverage of every unscripted order
  • Talent and producer networks: who worked where

Human checkpointYour head of sales picks the path. The note goes out from a person.

The story finder

“Across 412 hours of dailies, find every moment where the two leads disagree about money, and rank them by tension.”

  • Transcripts and speaker labels for every camera
  • Field producers’ logs and story notes
  • The episode outlines and the network’s notes
  • Releases and clearances for everyone on screen

Brand match

“Which ten brands should hear first about our cooking format, and why each one?”

  • The studio’s past brand partners and how they renewed
  • Category spend and brand-funded content in the trades
  • Audience fit from the format’s pilot and social data
  • Agency and CMO relationships across the team

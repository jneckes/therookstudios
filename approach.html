Phase 1 · Questions we answer

Five sets of questions, answered with evidence.

Your studio today

  1. What have you already made that buyers still talk about?
  2. Which of your people want to make more, and make something different?

Buyers

  1. Who is buying what you make, right now?
  2. Who holds the budget, and how fast do deals close?

Competition

  1. Who sells what you want to sell today?
  2. Where are the openings?

Ownership

  1. Which deals let you keep IP or format rights?
  2. Who else can fund the shows you own?

Economics

  1. How do margins compare with your core work?
  2. What mix of projects reaches the target?

Phase 2 · Staffing

There are three ways to staff a new unit.

Lean team inside

1–3 people

Low cost and a fast start. Development can stall when the day job gets busy.

Senior leader plus hires

3–6 people

The strongest slate. The highest fixed cost.

Partnership or joint venture

1–2 + partner

Speed and reach from a partner’s audience or IP. Shared control.

Our usual starting point

Start lean, with The Rook as interim lead. Hire a permanent head once the offer has proven itself in the market.

Room to go further

The Rook is open to a bigger role, such as longer-term interim leadership or a share in IP, where the economics line up.
